## 3. Tangible Benefits

| Benefit | Estimated Value |
|---------|----------------|
| Reduced average commuter wait time by ~8 minutes/trip | High — time is money |
| Fewer missed buses due to real-time tracking | Reduced productivity loss |
| Reduced need for printed schedules / tarpaulins | ~₱2,000 saved per terminal/year |
| Admin dashboard reduces manual schedule updates | ~5 hrs/week saved per operator |

---

## 4. Intangible Benefits

- Improved commuter confidence and satisfaction with public transport
- Increased transparency and trust in the transit system
- Demonstrates feasibility of smart city features in CDO
- Educational value — team gained real software engineering experience
- Potential to scale into a city-wide transit platform

---

## 5. ROI Calculation

```
Total Development Cost:     ₱21,000 (one-time)
Monthly Operational Cost:   ₱50/month
Annual Operational Cost:    ₱600/year

Estimated Annual Benefit:
  - Schedule printing saved:     ₱2,000 × 5 terminals = ₱10,000
  - Admin time saved (5 hrs/wk): 260 hrs × ₱150 = ₱39,000
  - Total Annual Benefit:        ₱49,000

ROI (Year 1) = (₱49,000 - ₱21,600) / ₱21,600 × 100 = ~127%
Break-even point: ~6 months after deployment
```

---

## 6. Case Study

> "If BusTrack is adopted across 10 bus terminals in Cagayan de Oro serving approximately 15,000 daily commuters, and each commuter saves an average of 8 minutes per trip, the system would recover over 2,000 person-hours of productive time daily. At an average informal wage of ₱60/hour, this represents approximately ₱120,000 of economic value returned to commuters every day — or roughly ₱3.6 million per month citywide."

---

## 7. Recommendation

**✅ Proceed with deployment and further development.**

BusTrack demonstrates a strong ROI of ~127% in Year 1 with near-zero ongoing operational costs using free-tier infrastructure. The intangible benefits — commuter trust, city transparency, and scalability — further justify continued investment. The team recommends pursuing a pilot deployment with 2–3 real bus terminals before a full citywide rollout.
